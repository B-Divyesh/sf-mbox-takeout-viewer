Privacy

Effective 27 August 2026. Paper Trail is designed so your email archive never leaves your device.

What stays local

The MBOX file is read directly by your browser. Message indexes, archive metadata, and your license token are stored in browser storage on this device. We do not receive message bodies, headers, attachment names, search terms, filenames, or index data.

Network requests

The static app and generated illustration load from this site. If you buy or verify a bulk-export license, your browser contacts api.sociobot.in with the license token. Checkout is handled by Sociobot/Dodo as merchant of record under their policies. The app has no advertising, behavioral analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party fonts.

Your control

Delete a site’s stored data through your browser settings to remove saved indexes and the local license. Removing an index never deletes or changes your source archive. Contact: privacy@sociobot.in.
